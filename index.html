PRODUCT THINKING

Why this prototype?

Fantasy managers rarely lack information. The harder problem is converting conflicting signals into a decision they can trust. This MVP makes the decision trace visible: users can see which factors helped or hurt each player and how risk tolerance changes the recommendation.
